# Incident Report: Ransomware Attack
## Summary

On [Date], our organization was targeted by a ransomware attack. The attackers encrypted various files on our file server, demanding payment in exchange for the decryption key. Our incident response team quickly responded to contain the spread of the malware and worked towards recovery.

## Root Cause

Based on the evidence, it appears that the attackers used a brute-force attack to gain access to our system (T1110). The entry vector is likely an exposed RDP connection or phishing attempt.

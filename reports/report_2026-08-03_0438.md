**Incident Report**

### Summary
A ransomware incident was detected on June 12, 2026, affecting a Windows RDP jump host. The attackers exploited brute-force login attempts to gain access to the system. A ransom note was left on the desktop, indicating that the attacker demanded payment for data decryption. However, our organization policy dictates against paying ransoms.

### Root Cause
The entry vector used by the attackers was brute-force login attempts against the `administrator` account. The attacker IP address (`91.219.236.18`) is still unknown.
